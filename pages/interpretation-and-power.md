<p class="subheading"><em>Who decides what messages mean?</em></p>

Stuart Hall's encoding/decoding framework examines how audiences interpret messages that are created to reflect a specific meaning. **Encoding** refers to the senders process of turning meaning into a message. **Decoding** is the audience interpreting the message.

The influence of **power** comes in where some entities have **greater influence** over how messages can be interpreted or delivered. This can include large media corporations, who often shape the narrative around breaking stpries and headlines, or even AI platforms like Open AI, who train their models with carefully cultivated data.

**In AI-mediated argumets,** the user must consider whether the response given by the chatbot fairly considered the opposing argument, or if it simply affirmed the feelings that were shared initially by the user.

<div class="page-callout">
  <strong>The Takeaway</strong>
  <p>Interpretation can never be entirely neutral. Whether the interpretation comes from oneself, an AI chatbot or another human, users must consider whos interpretation is reiforced, whos is being dismissed and why.</p>
</div>
