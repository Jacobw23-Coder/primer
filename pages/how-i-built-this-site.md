**Layout, Styles and Page Structure** 

- For ease of use with my website, I chose to format it with numbered sections, allowing the audience to navigate through in a way that fits the ideas discussed in my primer. Each section builds onto the one before it, making the website comprehensive and thorough. 
- Stylistically, I directed AI to incorporate a natural color palette that is visually cohesive, using greens, browns and yellow shades to complete the look. Additionaly, after refining the general direction I was seeking through many versions, I finally refined the look to be contemporary, fun and easy to use.
