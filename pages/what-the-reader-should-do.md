<p class="subheading"><em>Think critically, question appropriately, communicate responsibly</em></p>

**Effective communication in arguments** is about more than talking to another person. There is complexity at every step from encoding one's own message and delivering it through a channel, to recieving and decoding. It is important to also consider the immense context and experience that two people as individuals are subject to.

**What To Do:**

**1) Question your own interpretation of the argument first.**

<div class="page-callout">
  <strong>The Takeaway</strong>
  <p>As the different models and theories have shown, it is important to not take the commonplace action of communication for granted. Consider semiotics, the transmission model, encoding/decoding and the contexts of others before turning to an AI chatbot for clarification or interpretation. We as humans possess a superpower in this regard. Use it effectively.</p>
</div>
